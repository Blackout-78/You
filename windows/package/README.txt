You for Windows

To start:
  1. Double-click You.exe.

To put You on your Desktop and Start menu (optional):
  1. Double-click "Install shortcuts.bat".
  (Inside the program, Settings > Profile > "Make a shortcut with my picture" makes one with your own picture.)

To remove You:
  1. Close You.
  2. Double-click "Uninstall You.bat".
  3. Answer Y to confirm.
  4. Answer Y or N to also delete what You saved (library list, favorites, playlists, colors, settings).

If Windows says "Windows protected your PC": click "More info", then "Run anyway".

You collects no data and never connects to the internet.
